# Assumptions and reference interpretation

**Reference features modelled:** wayfarer-style rectangular front with rounded corners, matte-black frame, two front camera apertures at the outer corners, thickened outer corners, central bridge with nose arch and nose pads, straight temples with curved ear tips, small status indicator window on the outer face of one temple, dark optical panes; separate shield with continuous upper section following the frame contour, side walls, lower coverage, soft facial interface, magnet mounts at the frame corners.

**Interpretations / departures (all provisional):**
- The indicator is an inset window on the +X temple. Its function is undefined: it is NOT given a defined light behaviour beyond a dim material.
- Dark optical panes are placeholders: no display, optics or passthrough function is modelled or claimed.
- Shield extent: the side walls stop at x=+/-68 so they clear the temples; at the top/bottom they follow the frame outline.
- Shield hinge-sweep slots (12 mm tall) added to satisfy temple movement; light leak there unresolved.
- Additional top magnets (x=+/-28) are not visible in the reference; retention needs validation.
- Shield 'upper section' is not raised above the frame as the reference render appears to show: the shield stays within the frame outline.
- Magnet envelopes (4.5 x 4.5 x 1.5 mm) are placeholders; no magnet selected; no force value claimed.
- Face wrap (R=110, offset 12) is an assumed cylinder.
- No camera module, display, IMU, wiring or battery is modelled; their parameters are UNRESOLVED.
- Nothing here is manufacturing-ready or physically validated.
