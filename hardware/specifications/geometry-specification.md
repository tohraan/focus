# FOCUS geometry specification (v2)

Single source of truth: `scripts/params.py` -> `specifications/dimensions.csv`, embedded `Params` spreadsheet in every FCStd, CAD, blueprints, renders. CAD revision **GEO-c0371c81ad**.

## Coordinate system
X + = wearer's right, Y + toward face (front plane y=0), Z up, mm. (Left-handed triad, see geometry-report.) Frame centred on X=0; bridge centred on X=0.

## Verified reference dimensions
None verified. Reference-image labels (146 width, 44 height, 18 bridge, 52 lens width, 150 temple) are **PROVISIONAL**: the image is an AI-generated infographic. A 42 mm label on its side view is undefined and unused.

## Conventions
- Temple length = hinge axis to the rear-most point of the temple tip, measured along Y.
- Bridge width = gap between the two lens openings at the front plane. Lens opening width/height at the front plane.

## Constraints (as built)
- Constrained, spreadsheet-driven FreeCAD sketches: frame outline and two lens openings (tangent/horizontal/vertical/equal-radius/dimensional constraints bound to `Params`), extruded and cut as `Core_ParametricSlab` in `frame.FCStd`. Verified equal to the script geometry and responding to parameter edits.
- All other features (corner blocks, hinges, pockets, temples, shield) are generated by `build_cad.py` from the same table: **script-parametric, not sketch-constrained**; editing the spreadsheet in the GUI does not move them (edit `params.py` and rebuild).
- Shield contour derived from the frame outline by a shared function and Boolean (not a FreeCAD linked reference).
- Frame/shield magnet pockets and pins share the same parameter coordinates.
